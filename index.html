Our story

From 1942 to today

  1. 1942

    The school is founded

    K. S. Perumal Chettiar opens the school to bring accessible, quality education to the children of Pannaipuram.

  2. Honoured as a Best Teacher

    The founder receives the Nallasiriyar (Best Teacher) Award for his service to education.

  3. 2026

    Still teaching the village

    100+ students and 8 teachers carry the founder's vision forward, Classes 1 to 8, in Tamil medium.
